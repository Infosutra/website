Our Team

A Collective of Practitioners and Learners

Infosutra is shaped by people who bring care, curiosity, and long experience to their work. Our team includes practitioners, researchers, and advisors who have spent years working across development domains, technology, research, and public systems—learning from communities and translating that learning into thoughtful action.

Collective of Practitioners

Infosutra | Collective of Practitioners

Infosutra is a collective of seasoned development practitioners, researchers, and policy advisors with deep, practice-led experience across education, gender justice, health, livelihoods, agriculture, and social policy. Our work is grounded in long-term field engagement, collaborative partnerships, and a commitment to equity, inclusion, and evidence-informed system strengthening. Together, we bring complementary strengths that bridge community realities, research rigour, and policy processes.
