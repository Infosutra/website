Entrepreneurship

Supporting Pathways to Livelihoods and Local Enterprise

We work alongside aspiring entrepreneurs, startups, and local institutions to support livelihoods, enterprise growth, and resilient local economies. Our focus is on strengthening people, ideas, and ecosystems so entrepreneurship can be inclusive, sustainable, and rooted in community realities.

Our Approach to Entrepreneurship

We see entrepreneurship as more than business creation—it is about confidence, capability, and connection. Our work supports individuals and groups to develop skills, access resources, and navigate pathways from ideas to viable enterprises. Through incubation support, mentoring, peer learning, and partnerships with financial institutions and networks, we help entrepreneurs grow ventures that generate livelihoods and contribute to local development.

Areas of Work

Skills Development & Innovation

Supporting essential business skills such as financial literacy, marketing, planning, and problem-solving—while encouraging creativity and locally relevant innovation.

Startup Support & Mentorship

Connecting entrepreneurs with mentors, practitioners, and peers to refine ideas, navigate challenges, and grow with confidence.

Access to Finance & Markets

Supporting entrepreneurs to explore funding options—grants, loans, and alternative finance—alongside financial literacy and market linkages.

Social Entrepreneurship & Impact

Encouraging enterprises that respond to social and environmental challenges, balancing economic viability with positive community impact.

How We Support Entrepreneurship Work

Program Design & Development

Co-creating entrepreneurship programs tailored to specific groups, sectors, and local contexts.

Curriculum & Resource Development

Designing practical learning content on enterprise skills, leadership, and impact-oriented entrepreneurship.

Training & Capacity Building

Facilitating hands-on workshops and learning spaces to support enterprise launch and sustainability.

Mentorship & Networks

Building mentorship pathways and peer networks that support learning and growth.

Finance & Market Linkages

Supporting access to finance and helping entrepreneurs connect with relevant markets and value chains.

Learning & Impact Assessment

Reflecting on outcomes, sustainability, and longer-term livelihood impacts to strengthen programs.

Agri-Entrepreneurship
