Multi-Sectoral Development

Working Across Sectors to Support Community-Led Change

We work alongside communities, local institutions, and public systems to support development that reflects how people experience life across education, livelihoods, health, and the environment. Our multi-sectoral work recognises that lasting change emerges when efforts are connected, contextual, and community-led.

Our Approach

We begin by listening closely to communities and partners—understanding priorities, existing strengths, and local systems. Our approach is participatory and inclusive, supporting communities to shape solutions that matter to them.

By working with local organisations, government bodies, and community leaders, we support integrated efforts that address social, economic, and environmental dimensions together—strengthening resilience and long-term wellbeing.
