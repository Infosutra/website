Education

Strengthening Learning Ecosystems, Together

We work alongside educators, communities, and institutions to strengthen education systems that are inclusive, equitable, and responsive to context. Our focus is on supporting learning environments where children and young people can grow with confidence, curiosity, and care.

Our Approach to Education

We believe meaningful change in education happens when learning is supported at multiple levels—from classrooms to systems. Our work centres on capacity building, reflective practice, and collaboration, recognising the roles of teachers, families, communities, and institutions.

Through thoughtful pedagogy, appropriate use of technology, and deep community engagement, we support efforts to ensure that every learner has access to quality education that prepares them for changing realities.

Areas of Work in Education

Early Childhood Care & Education (ECCE)

Supporting nurturing care and foundational learning in the early years (0–5), with attention to cognitive, emotional, and physical development.

Foundational Literacy & Numeracy (FLN)

Strengthening core reading, writing, and numeracy skills to build strong foundations for lifelong learning.

STEM Learning

Encouraging curiosity, critical thinking, and problem-solving through integrated science, technology, engineering, and mathematics learning.

21st Century Skills

Supporting learners to develop skills such as digital literacy, collaboration, communication, and adaptability.

Learning Recovery

Designing targeted support to help learners regain confidence and progress following learning disruptions.

Community Engagement

Working with parents and communities to strengthen accountability, trust, and shared responsibility in education.
